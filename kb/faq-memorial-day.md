# Is CalColor open on Memorial Day?

Yes. CalColor operates on a weekly class schedule and does not close for long weekends, so classes run as usual on Memorial Day. The only exception is the Newark campus, which is closed every Monday as part of its regular schedule, including Monday holidays.

CalColor's only scheduled closures are full-week breaks (Spring Break, Thanksgiving, and Christmas/New Year), so unless a date falls inside one of those breaks, regular classes continue on the normal campus schedule.
